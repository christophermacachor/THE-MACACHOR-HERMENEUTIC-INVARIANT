### Governing principle

> **No semantic claim may be treated as fundamental unless its
> structural provenance is identified.**

### Epistemic status

Every claim in this repository is classified as one of:

-   **E --- Established:** supported by established mathematics,
    physics, information theory, or independent empirical evidence.
-   **M --- Macachor Formalism:** a definition introduced by the
    framework.
-   **H --- Hypothesis:** an empirical or physical claim proposed for
    testing.
-   **A --- Analogy:** a correspondence between domains that is not
    asserted as identity.

### Core pipeline

`SUBSTRATE → FIRST DERIVATIVE → COHERENCE → VECTOR → REPRESENTATION → SEMANTICS → VERIFICATION`

### Scope

The framework proposes that structural information can be analyzed
before its decomposition into conventional symbols, measurements, and
semantic categories.

It does **not** claim that the proposed scalar substrate is already an
experimentally established physical field of meaning.

Quantum mappings in the paper are explicitly treated as proposed
mathematical correspondences unless independently derived from physical
theory and experiment.

### AI research hypothesis

The framework proposes that hallucination can be studied as a problem of
underdetermined reconstruction:

> If a system lacks sufficient structural constraints to uniquely
> determine a target claim, statistically plausible but structurally
> false outputs should become more likely.

This is a research hypothesis, not a claim that hallucination is
mathematically inevitable in every AI architecture.

### Coherence Gate

Before accepting a downstream interpretation, ask:

1.  What is the substrate?
2.  What is the first structural transformation?
3.  What preserves coherence with the substrate?
4.  What vector or directional constraint was introduced?
5.  What representation was produced?
6.  What semantic interpretation was assigned?
7.  What evidence supports the claim?
8.  Can the representation be traced backward to its structural
    provenance?
